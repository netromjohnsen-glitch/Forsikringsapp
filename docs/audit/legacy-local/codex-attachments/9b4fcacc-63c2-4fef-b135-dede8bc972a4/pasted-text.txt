==================================================
20 – UNKNOWN ACCEPTANCE
==================================================

Unknown is acceptable when:

A.
Applicable sources genuinely do not document the fact safely.

B.
Value is customer-specific.

C.
Concept is provider-specific and symmetry would be misleading.

D.
Sources are ambiguous/conflicting.

E.
Detail is intentionally outside advisor-relevant modeled scope.

Unknown is NOT justified merely because catalog currently lacks a fact.
